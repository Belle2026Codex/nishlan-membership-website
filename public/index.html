OVERVIEW

Welcome to Nishlan

Founded on February 18, 2023, Nishlan FoodLife & Marketing Inc. is a Davao-based food distribution and retail marketing business focused on making imported chocolates and sweets accessible to more households, retailers and resellers.

Affordable favorites

Imported chocolates and sweets selected with value in mind.

Retail & wholesale

Products for everyday shoppers, bulk buyers and resellers.

Growing across Mindanao

Bringing quality treats closer to more communities.

ABOUT US

Our vision & mission

Our Vision

To become a leading chocolate distributor and value-focused food marketing company in Mindanao, known for affordability, quality products and dependable service.

Our Mission

To make quality chocolates and snacks affordable and accessible through trusted suppliers, strong partnerships and customer-focused service.
