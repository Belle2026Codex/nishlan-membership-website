OVERVIEW

Welcome to Nishlan

Founded on February 18, 2023, Nishlan FoodLife & Marketing Inc. is a Davao-based food distribution and retail marketing business focused on making imported chocolates and sweets accessible to more households, retailers and resellers.

Affordable favorites

Imported chocolates and sweets selected with value in mind.

Retail & wholesale

Products for everyday shoppers, bulk buyers and resellers.

Growing across Mindanao

Bringing quality treats closer to more communities.

ABOUT US

Our vision & mission

Our Vision

To become a leading chocolate distributor and value-focused food marketing company in Mindanao, known for affordability, quality products and dependable service.

Our Mission

To make quality chocolates and snacks affordable and accessible through trusted suppliers, strong partnerships and customer-focused service.

HOW IT WORKS

From application to your digital membership card

01

Complete the online form

Enter your full name, email address, mobile number and preferred membership category. No password or account creation is required.

02

Nishlan reviews your application

Our administrator checks your details, membership eligibility and any applicable payment requirements. Submission does not mean automatic approval.

03

Receive an email update

After a decision, an email notification is intended to be sent to the address you registered. Please use a valid email you can access.

04

Get and present your QR card

Once approved and payment requirements are met, you'll be assigned a membership number and a unique QR code to present for verification at Nishlan branches.

What do I receive when approved?

A unique Nishlan membership number and digital QR card. Discounts or other privileges depend on the membership program's finalized terms and branch eligibility.
