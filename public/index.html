OVERVIEW

Welcome to Nishlan

Founded on February 18, 2023, Nishlan FoodLife & Marketing Inc. is a Davao-based food distribution and retail marketing business focused on making imported chocolates and sweets accessible to more households, retailers and resellers.

Affordable favorites

Retail & wholesale

Growing across Mindanao

Bringing quality treats closer to more communities.

WHY BECOME A MEMBER?

More than a card. A better way to connect with Nishlan.

Membership gives you a personal QR card, a 3% purchase discount, and additional benefits depending on your membership category.

◈

General and Eagles members receive a 3% discount on total purchases under their approved membership. General membership is renewed annually and includes a yearly surprise raffle; Eagles membership is lifetime and provides an additional 3% purchase-based allocation to Eagles Club funding.

01 · EVERYDAY SHOPPERS

General Membership

Enjoy 3% off your total purchases as a General Member, plus entry into Nishlan's yearly surprise raffle for eligible members.

Validity: 1 year. Membership must be renewed every year to continue enjoying the benefits.

02 · PHILIPPINE EAGLES MEMBERS

Eagles Membership

Verified Philippine Eagles members enjoy 3% off their total purchases. An additional 3% of the purchase amount goes toward Eagles Club funding, separate from the member's discount.

Lifetime membership. No annual renewal is required. The Eagles Club contribution is not an additional customer discount.

HOW IT WORKS

From application to your digital membership card

01

Complete the online form

Enter your full name, email address, mobile number and preferred membership category. No password or account creation is required.

02

Nishlan reviews your application

Our administrator checks your details, membership eligibility and any applicable payment requirements. Submission does not mean automatic approval.

03

Receive an email update

After a decision, an email notification is intended to be sent to the address you registered. Please use a valid email you can access.

04

Get and present your QR card

Once approved and payment requirements are met, you'll be assigned a membership number and a unique QR code to present for verification at Nishlan branches.

What do I receive when approved?

A unique Nishlan membership number and digital QR card. General members receive a 3% purchase discount and yearly surprise raffle benefit, with annual renewal. Eagles members receive a 3% purchase discount plus a separate 3% purchase-based Eagles Club funding allocation, with lifetime validity.
